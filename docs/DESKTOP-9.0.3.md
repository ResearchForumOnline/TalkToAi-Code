# TalkToAi Code 9.0.3

## Pick a specialist or leave it on Auto

Agent Zero runs Chat and Code. Leave **Agent Zero specialist** on **Auto** to choose guidance from your request. Pick **Games**, **Coding**, **Research**, **Writing**, **Shopping**, or **Everyday** when you want a specific approach. TalkToAi remembers that choice in the current conversation. A new conversation starts on Auto.

| Specialist | Try asking |
| --- | --- |
| **Games** | “Review gameplay, tests and asset licenses in this Godot project.” |
| **Coding** | “Fix the loader and investigate gaps in its regression tests.” |
| **Research** | “Review sources, methods and claims in my research project.” |
| **Writing** | “Revise the project draft and check its facts and terminology.” |
| **Shopping** | “Find a 2 TB SSD under £100 and compare delivery and returns.” |
| **Everyday** | “Help me organize my week.” |

For independent questions in a local project, Zero can launch up to three separate read-only reviewers on the selected tool-capable Ollama model. A game team can inspect gameplay, playtests, and asset licenses. A research team can inspect evidence, methods, and claims. The reviewers run one at a time to share the same loaded model. Zero brings their reports back into the task and handles edits or checks within the selected project.

Shopping comparisons work with available browser search and reading on public seller pages. Zero can check the product variant, condition, total price, delivery, and returns. The comparison includes the seller links so you can review the options and decide.
